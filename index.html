Our story

Cut from a
different cloth.

Margiela Coffee started as a single counter and a single question: what happens when you make coffee the way a good tailor makes a garment — every seam considered, nothing added for show.

We keep the list short, source beans from growers we can name, and finish every cup by hand. What you see is what's in it — raw, exact, unfussy.
